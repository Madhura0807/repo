Research Highlights & Methodology

  • MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning: Leveraged lightweight depthwise separable convolutions in MobileNetV2, fine-tuning pre-trained weights to classify agricultural leaf diseases with high precision and minimal computational overhead.

model = models.Sequential([
    base_model,
